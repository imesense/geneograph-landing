Geneograph boards

Not every branch is certain.

Genealogy research often lives somewhere between “I know” and “I think.” Geneograph gives you a visual space to explore possible relationships, compare theories and organize evidence without treating every idea as a confirmed fact.

A normal family tree represents what I currently believe to be true.

Product direction

Your family history should be yours.

GeneoGraph is being designed as a local-first application, allowing the core family-history workspace to live on your own computer instead of making cloud storage mandatory.

01
