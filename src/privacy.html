Privacy at GeneoGraph

This notice covers the GeneoGraph website, contact form, Research Group signup and interactive demo.

Updated 24 September 2026

Research Group signup

The form asks for your email address so we can contact you about the GeneoGraph Research Group. Web3Forms processes your submission and delivers it to GeneoGraph’s inbox. We keep your contact email for this purpose until you ask us to remove it or the Research Group ends.

Contact messages

The contact form asks for your email address, a subject and a question so we can respond. Web3Forms processes and forwards the message to GeneoGraph’s inbox. We keep the correspondence as needed to answer and follow up on your question, unless you ask us to remove it.

Services used by this site

Web3Forms may retain submission data and technical records under its privacy policy. GitHub Pages hosts this website and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. We do not run advertising or analytics scripts on this landing website.

Your choices

To stop Research Group contact or ask us to remove your email from our contact records, write to geneograph@gmail.com. Service-provider records and backups follow their own retention policies.

Interactive demo

Changes you make to the sample project in the interactive demo happen in your browser. GeneoGraph does not collect or save those research changes, and they reset when you reload the demo. The demo may keep limited preferences, such as your language and last-opened project, in your browser. If you use map search, your search request is sent to MapTiler to return place results.
