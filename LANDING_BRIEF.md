# Landing Surface Brief

## Mode

Persuade

## Direction contract

- **FORM seed:** The Living Research Desk—an editorial landing surface organized around a large, working Geneograph canvas rather than a conventional marketing hero.
- **First viewport:** An explicit genealogy category, the two-part organize/visualize proposition, one dominant Demo action, and an inspectable research board showing people, evidence, an unknown person, and a possible relationship.
- **Visitor path:** Product category and promise → uncertainty model → connected research context → working board to clear custom diagram → audience and ownership → Demo → research group → origin.
- **Signature moment:** The dashed possible relationship draws into place once while remaining visible by default; reduced-motion disables it.
- **Build path:** Code-led for this implementation. No standing project default has been recorded.
- **Responsive rule:** Recompose and fit product canvases inside narrow viewports; never rely on unreadable desktop-scale cropping.

## Boundaries

- Preserve the charcoal and GeneoGraph-green identity, logo, static Vite architecture, accessibility foundations, and separate demo application.
- Demo links target `https://demo.geneograph.com`.
- Do not invent testimonials, metrics, founder details, or production capabilities.
- Signup remains an honest configurable prototype form until a real endpoint is supplied.
